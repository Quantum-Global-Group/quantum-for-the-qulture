# Lesson template

Copy this structure into a notebook section or a short reading. Delete the parenthetical hints. Lab 001 is the worked example.

One idea per section. If you need a second definition, start a new section.

---

# Section title

## What are we doing?

One or two sentences. Name the object the learner will have at the end.

## Why are we doing it?

The question this section answers, and why the previous section was not enough.

## How does it work?

The definition, then one example. A diagram or a vector belongs here if the idea is spatial or algebraic. A gate needs its name, its symbol, what goes in, what comes out, and why that matters.

PAUSE HERE if the learner should predict before the example runs.

## What happened?

The number, vector, or drawing the code produced. Refer to the output. Do not retype a result you did not run.

## What does it mean?

The claim you can defend, and the claim you cannot. If a common misreading exists, state the accurate reading in a positive sentence and then name the misreading.

CHECK YOUR UNDERSTANDING. One question whose ingredients are already on the page.

## Where can I go next?

The next section, or a single official page.

FIND IT IN THE DOCS. Page title, URL, and the one thing to find there.

TRY THIS, only when a one-line change teaches the boundary of the idea.
