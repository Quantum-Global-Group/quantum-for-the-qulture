# Certification journey

This page is an orientation to an external credential. It is not a course, and it is not a claim that Lab 001 prepares you to pass an exam.

Checked on 18 November 2026 by opening IBM's certification page for credential C9008400:

[IBM Certified Quantum Computation using Qiskit v2.X Developer - Associate](https://www.ibm.com/training/certification/ibm-certified-quantum-computation-using-qiskit-v2x-developer-associate-C9008400)

That page listed the credential as Live. It said the required exam is Fundamentals of Quantum Computing Using Qiskit v2.X Developer, with 68 questions, 47 required to pass, and 90 minutes. It said this credential replaces C0010300. The older Qiskit v0.2X associate credential page said that certification was withdrawn on 30 September 2025. Do not study the withdrawn exam as if it were current. Confirm the live page again before you pay for an exam, because IBM can change the details.

## How Lab 001 relates

The live page's public topic list includes quantum operations, visualizing circuits and states, basic circuits, running circuits, the sampler primitive, the estimator primitive, and reading results. Lab 001 gives you one careful example of several of those ideas: a two-qubit circuit, a local sampler, a local estimator, and a local transpilation.

The same page also includes dynamic circuits, parameterized circuits, execution modes, primitive options, job monitoring, and OpenQASM 3. The beginner notebook shows one `assign_parameters` call and one Sampler PUB with two angles. That is a single example. Lab 001 does not teach dynamic circuits, execution modes, primitive options, job monitoring, or OpenQASM 3. A person who wants the credential still needs the official objectives and a broader set of practice than this repository currently contains. A score inside the beginner notebook is practice for that lesson. It is not the exam.

Passing the exam is optional. It is one way to demonstrate Qiskit skill to someone who recognizes the credential. It is not a requirement of this pathway, and it is not a job offer.

## How later material should be organized

When more labs exist, a certification track can map each public exam section to a lab, and can mark sections this pathway does not cover. That map should quote the official section titles and link the official page, rather than inventing objective numbers. Until those labs exist, the map would be a list of gaps. The gaps are already stated above.

## What we will not publish

Retired objective lists presented as current. Practice questions written to imitate a live exam. Any claim that a learner who finishes Lab 001 is an IBM certified quantum computational scientist.
