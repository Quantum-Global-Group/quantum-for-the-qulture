# Career pathways

This is an orientation, not a hiring guide. Completing Lab 001 does not qualify you for any role below. The lab shows that you can follow one Bell state from a definition to a local measurement. Employers and graduate programs ask for more, and they ask for different things.

Use the three columns this way.

- Prerequisites: what you already need before training for the role is a good use of time.
- Transferable skills: strengths from software, laboratory work, teaching, or operations that still matter.
- Role-specific training: work this repository does not replace.

The Quantum Career Navigator on the Quantum Global Group website is a separate tool. It is not this page, and it is not something this repository implements.

## Roles

### Quantum software

Prerequisites: you can write and test programs in a general-purpose language. Transferable skills: API design, debugging, and reading other people's code. Role-specific training: Qiskit or another SDK in production use, plus the algorithms a team actually runs. Lab 001 is one exercise inside the SDK, not that body of work.

### Quantum hardware

Prerequisites: comfort with laboratory measurement or with electronic systems. Transferable skills: careful logs, calibration habits, and respect for instruments. Role-specific training: the device physics and the control stack of one modality. A notebook does not substitute for time on a real apparatus.

### Control systems

Prerequisites: signals, feedback, or embedded software. Transferable skills: timing, noise, and the discipline of not changing two things at once. Role-specific training: the control electronics and software used by a specific quantum platform.

### Algorithms

Prerequisites: linear algebra and the habit of proving a small case by hand. Transferable skills: complexity, numerics, and writing a claim so someone else can check it. Role-specific training: particular algorithms and their assumptions. Lab 001 stops at one state and three expectation values.

### Applications

Prerequisites: a domain where a computational problem already exists, such as chemistry, optimization, or data analysis. Transferable skills: knowing the classical baseline and the cost of being wrong. Role-specific training: mapping that problem onto a quantum method, and comparing them. This lab does not show an application advantage. It says so in the notebook.

### Cybersecurity

Prerequisites: classical security fundamentals. Transferable skills: threat modeling and credential hygiene. Role-specific training: post-quantum cryptography, or the security of quantum systems, depending on which job you mean. Those are different trainings. Lab 001's only security lesson is how not to handle an account secret, which is necessary and not sufficient.

### Educator

Prerequisites: you can teach a group and you are willing to learn the mathematics you assign. Transferable skills: lesson design, questioning, and classroom management. Role-specific training: the science in enough depth to answer the second question, plus practice with the tools. The instructor guide is a plan for one lab, not a teaching credential.

### Scientific computing

Prerequisites: numerical computing and reproducible environments. Transferable skills: testing, plotting with labeled axes, and separating a model from a measurement. Role-specific training: the scientific libraries and data a group uses. The smoke test in this repository is a small example of that habit.

### Technical project management

Prerequisites: you have shipped technical work with other people. Transferable skills: scope, risk, and an honest status. Role-specific training: enough quantum vocabulary to tell a research claim from a milestone. This page is a start on the vocabulary. It is not the project experience.

### Workforce development

Prerequisites: experience with learners or with hiring. Transferable skills: pathways, prerequisites, and removing needless barriers. Role-specific training: the difference between a public lab and a program that assesses job readiness. Quantum Global Group may offer deeper work of that kind later. The public name for one such concept is the Quantum Workforce Readiness Sprint. No offer is described here.

## A reasonable next step after Lab 001

Write which column you are strongest in today. Then pick one gap that is a prerequisite, and one piece of role-specific training you could start this month. If no role on this list is appealing, that is a useful result. The lab was still worth doing as science.
