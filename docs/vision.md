# Vision

Quantum for the Qulture is a hands-on pathway from quantum curiosity to quantum capability. It is an initiative of Quantum Global Group.

The public promise is short. A learner can see a quantum idea, learn the mathematics that gives the idea a precise meaning, represent it in Qiskit, run it locally, and, when access and judgment allow, run one deliberate job on a quantum computer. Then they can say what the result does and does not show, and where they might go next.

## Access without dilution

Access means a person can start with no IBM account and no laboratory seat. Dilution would mean skipping the mathematics so the topic feels friendly, or stacking undefined words so it sounds advanced. We do neither. Each lab introduces a term when the learner needs it, then asks them to calculate, code, and interpret.

Hardware is never the price of the foundations. A simulator result is labeled as a simulator result. A hardware result is collected only when a learner chooses that step and has access.

## Who it is for

The same first lab serves several readers. It does not try to be each reader's entire curriculum.

- A curious beginner who wants to know what entanglement actually looks like in numbers.
- A student who will meet this material in a course.
- A technical professional who can already program and wants the quantum objects named correctly.
- An educator who needs a worksheet, a timing plan, and a backup that does not depend on a device.
- An organization that wants to see how Quantum Global Group teaches before it asks for a deeper engagement.
- A career explorer who needs an honest map of roles, including the training this lab does not replace.

## Public education, and work that stays private

The pages and labs here are the public education layer. Quantum Global Group may later offer private capability-building. One name for that kind of engagement is the Quantum Workforce Readiness Sprint. It is a concept only. This repository does not list prices, clients, or delivery methods.

## A first release, on purpose

Lab 001 is the first complete lab. The later sequence is written down in [../ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) and is not built yet. We would rather have one lab a learner can run than a tree of empty courses.
